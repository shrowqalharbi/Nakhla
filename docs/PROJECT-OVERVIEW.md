# Nakhla | نخلة
## Project Overview & Business Analysis

### Project snapshot
Nakhla is an e-commerce course project focused on Saudi dates and personalized gift boxes.

- **Course:** E-Business (IS 1460)
- **University:** Imam Mohammad Ibn Saud Islamic University
- **Semester:** 1st Semester 2026–2027
- **Team:** Brooq Alghamdi, Shrowq Alharbi, and Lamya Almutairi
- **Website:** https://6af2cb9649b2f.site123.me/
- **Instagram:** https://www.instagram.com/nakhla.onlineshop/

### Business concept and value proposition
Nakhla offers Saudi dates for personal consumption and gifting. Customers can browse individual products, select ready-made gift boxes, or build a customized box by choosing date varieties and quantities, box size, packaging style, and a personal greeting-card message.

The value proposition is the combination of a focused Saudi-dates offering and a flexible gifting experience for different preferences, budgets, and occasions.

### Products and services
- Nine Saudi date varieties.
- Five ready-made gift-box options.
- Custom boxes in three sizes: 500 g, 1 kg, and 3 kg.
- Three packaging styles and personalized greeting cards.
- Product catalog, product details, cart, checkout interface, ratings, contact form, and policy pages.

### Business analysis

#### Target customer segments
1. **Individuals and families:** dates for personal consumption, hospitality, and family visits.
2. **Gifting customers:** gifts for Ramadan, Eid, graduations, celebrations, and social occasions.
3. **Local-product customers:** customers who value Saudi products and cultural identity.
4. **Corporate customers:** businesses purchasing gifts for employees, clients, or events.

#### Customer needs
- Convenient browsing and comparison of Saudi date varieties.
- Gift choices that match different occasions and budgets.
- Flexibility to choose date types, quantities, box size, packaging, and a greeting message.
- Clear product information, prices, delivery details, and customer policies.

#### Market and industry factors
- **Demographic:** customers of different genders across Saudi Arabia, including individuals, families, and businesses.
- **Economic:** product and customization choices support different price points.
- **Social and cultural:** dates are associated with Saudi hospitality, generosity, family visits, and gifting.
- **Seasonality:** demand may increase during Ramadan, Eid, graduations, and other occasions.

#### Competitors and positioning
The business plan identifies Tamrah (https://www.date.com.sa/) and Special Dates (https://specialdates.store/en-sa/) as competitors. Nakhla's intended differentiation is its focus on Saudi dates and the ability to customize a gift box across date selection, quantities, packaging, occasion, and personal message.

#### Business model and revenue model
- **Business model:** B2C online retail, selling directly to consumers. Corporate customers are also a target segment.
- **Revenue model:** sales revenue from individual date products and ready-made or customized gift boxes.

### Marketing and customer relationships
- Instagram content to showcase date varieties, gift-box designs, and offers.
- Seasonal campaigns around Ramadan, Eid, and other gifting occasions.
- 5% promotional coupon offers described in the business plan.
- Promotional collaborations with Creative Workshops and Ghars are described in the plan.
- Ratings, written reviews, questionnaires, and website analytics are intended to support service improvement.
- The stated customer-service policy aims to respond to email inquiries within 12 hours.

### Operations and technology
- **Website platform:** SITE123 using a Software as a Service (SaaS) model for website tools and hosting.
- **Payment gateway described in the business plan:** Moyasar.
- **Payment methods listed in the plan:** credit card, Apple Pay, and cash on delivery with a SAR 10 fee.
- **Fulfillment:** Hatik Express for storage, packing, and delivery; Aramex and SMSA are additional delivery providers.
- **Core functions:** catalog and categories, custom-box selection, price summary, cart, checkout, accounts, coupons, VAT calculation, reviews, and contact options.

### Risks and responses
| Risk | Planned response |
|---|---|
| Product quality or storage issues | Work with reliable suppliers, inspect products, and maintain suitable storage conditions. |
| Delivery delays or damaged boxes | Use reliable delivery providers, protective packaging, and order tracking. |
| Customization mistakes | Review selected dates, quantities, box size, packaging, and card message before preparation. |
| Seasonal demand increases | Plan inventory and packaging supplies in advance. |
| Customer data and security risks | Use HTTPS, secure authentication, restricted access, trusted payment methods, and clear privacy practices. |

### Business impact
- **Individuals:** makes date shopping and gifting more convenient and customizable.
- **Businesses:** creates an online channel for local suppliers and opportunities to work with packaging and delivery providers.
- **Society:** promotes Saudi dates and their connection to hospitality, generosity, and local heritage.
